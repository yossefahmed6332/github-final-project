# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates the simple interest earned on a principal amount over a given period of time at a specified interest rate.

## Formula

The simple interest is calculated using the following formula:

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

Where:

* **Principal (P)** = The initial amount of money.
* **Rate (R)** = The annual interest rate in percentage.
* **Time (T)** = The period of time in years.

## Example

If the principal is **$1,000**, the annual interest rate is **5%**, and the time period is **2 years**:

```text
Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100
                = $100
```

Therefore, the simple interest is **$100**.

## Usage

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. The calculator will calculate and display the simple interest.
